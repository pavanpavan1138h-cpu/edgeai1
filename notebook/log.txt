    4. Hardware Glitch Detection & Domain Analysis:
       a) battery_v == 0.0 V (9 rows)
          - Physical Reason: Li-ion cell operates between ~3.3V and 4.2V. 
            0.0V indicates ADC / brownout dropout during packet transmission.
       b) temp_c == -127.0 °C (5 rows)
          - Physical Reason: -127°C is a digital 1-Wire bus error code (0x80/0x81)

            0 dBm represents an unassociated/unconnected Wi-Fi driver default.
       d) Overlap Audit: Overlap between rules is exactly 0 (disjoint rows).
       e) Total Glitch Rows Dropped: 24 rows (1.20% of dataset).
       f) Clean Dataset Retained: 1,976 rows (98.80% of dataset).
    5. Valid Extremes Kept:

    6. Provisional Heuristic Disclaimer:

       - Detected 12 raw negative predictions (3.04% of validation cases, min = -1.38d).

       - 0–10 days window (N=85, 21.5% of val): MAE = 1.193d, Median AE = 1.035d.
       - > 120 days window (N=72): MAE = 8.66d.
       - Physical reason: heteroscedasticity due to the flat discharge plateau where
         dV/dt ≈ 0 makes fresh batteries noisier to separate from instantaneous telemetry.
